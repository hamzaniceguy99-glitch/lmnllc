Our services, in detail

Four moving services for Florida households, all estimated in writing after a survey.

Program 01 · Surveyed, then priced

Local & in-state moves

We survey the home — in person or by video — because the garage, the attic and the shed are where estimates go wrong. The written estimate states whether it is binding or non-binding and what would change it, so the figure on the day is not a surprise.

Program 02 · Or just the awkward things

Packing & unpacking

Most people do not need full packing. They need the kitchen, the artwork and the television done properly and will handle the rest. We will say which parts are worth paying for, and note that items packed by the owner are treated differently for liability than items we packed.
